# Bio capital cascade (Rod) — singular track

Locked 2026-09-25. Objective: compound biological businesses into land, subterranean vertical greens/algae, quail, aquaponics, then community QoL from surplus only.

## Order
1. **Worms** — cash, live feed, castings; forward-sell P10 surplus to local quail/duck/chicken farms
2. **Crickets + isopods (rollie pollies)** — denser protein / bioactive SKUs; small climate footprint
3. **Land + partially underground vertical greens + algae** — calories, loops, real estate basis
4. **Quail** — eggs/meat; consume own insects/greens (vertical integrate; farms were earlier customers)
5. **Aquaponics** — fish + plants on shared water/nutrient spine
6. **Community quality of life** — fund only from surplus after reserves

## Gates
| Stage | Enter | Unlock next |
|------:|-------|-------------|
| 1 | ~16.5k starters + climate bins (or grow from 30 with long clock) | ≥$2k/mo vermiculture revenue OR firm prepaid forward runway covering Stage-1 costs; Rod clears the gate |
| 2 | Stage-1 cash ≥ setup + 3 mo opex | Insect revenue ≥ worm contribution |
| 3 | 6 mo trailing cash covers land/lease + fit-out without killing insect float | First greens/algae cycle self-funding |
| 4 | On-site insect + greens feed reliable | Quail margin > feed cost |
| 5 | Quail + plant demand stable; power/water known | Aquaponics CF ≥ debt service |
| 6 | 6 mo opex reserve intact | Never raid breed stock or land reserves |

## Active now
**Stage 1 only.** Worms are the only active spend until vermiculture revenue is at least **$2k/mo**, or a firm prepaid forward runway covers Stage-1 costs. Agents must not open Stage 2+ spend (crickets, isopods, land, greens, algae, quail, aquaponics, community QoL) unless Rod clears that gate.

Population and forward-book models live outside this repository at `/workspace/worm-revenue-model/` (growth MC + forward book). Do not vendor that tree here. Do not open land, quail, or aquaponics spend until the Stage-1 gate is cleared.

## Synergy
One nutrient spine: waste → worms/isopods; insects+greens → quail/fish; water → greens; castings → plants. Own the stack; no SaaS-per-stage.

## Anti-patterns
- Parallel-launching stages 2–5 before Stage-1 cash
- Funding QoL from breeding stock sales
- Renting climate self-storage for live cultures (lease bans)
- Selling median (P50) herd forward — firm book = P10 surplus only
